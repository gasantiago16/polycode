# Deep research review

Job is to refute. "Looks fine" with no primary is a failed agent.

1. Extract numbered atomic claims from $ARGUMENTS (a path or pasted claims).
2. For each, web_search + web_fetch primaries. Verdict: holds | holds-with-caveat | fails | unknown.
3. Write a scorecard: claim, verdict, best URL, hole. What we must not say in a pitch.

Do not change product code. Report only.
